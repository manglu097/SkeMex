### Core Action
If a current working hypothesis is supported by multiple direct observations or prior evidence, do NOT replace it solely because a new hypothesis is associated with background risk factors or loose pattern matches. Instead: (1) check whether any new data explicitly contradicts the current hypothesis or directly supports the alternative; (2) if no such evidence exists, keep the original hypothesis as the primary working explanation; (3) record the alternative only as a secondary possibility to monitor; (4) switch the primary hypothesis only after new observations or tool outputs provide stronger or contradictory evidence.
